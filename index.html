    How to Play

    Gollachut is a Bangladeshi village chase game. Runners must break through four defender lines to reach the safe zone — defenders must stop them.

    🎯 The Goal

    • A match is best of 3 rounds; sides swap every round.
    • Each round lasts 75 seconds after a short line-up.
    • Whoever wins 2 rounds takes the match.

    🏃 When You Run

    • Start at the base and cross all four defender lines.
    • Reach the safe zone at the far end to score.
    • Get tagged and you are held — your captain can touch you free.

    🛡️ When You Defend

    • You own one line and can only lean a little off it.
    • Tag runners as they try to slip past you.
    • Don't camp one spot — patrol your line or you slow down.

    ⚡ Roles & Ultimate

    • Pick a runner role and a defender role before the match.
    • Each role has its own ultimate — hover a role to read it.
    • The ultimate bar fills over time and on near-misses and tags.
